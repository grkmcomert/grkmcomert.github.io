Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the Rythmo mobile app (the "App") processes and why. The App is an educational app that teaches ECG rhythms. It does not ask you to create an account or provide an email address, phone number, location or health data.

1. Data we process and why

DataPurposeWhere
The username you choose, XP, day streak, weekly XP and last update timeTo show the weekly and all-time leaderboardsGoogle Firebase (Cloud Firestore)
A random, anonymous user IDSo that only you can update your leaderboard entryGoogle Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in)
Purchase and subscription status, an anonymous app user IDTo verify and restore your Rythmo Pro membershipApple App Store, RevenueCat
Device and advertising data (e.g. advertising ID, IP address, device model)To show ads in the free version and measure themGoogle AdMob
Lesson progress, stars, settings, reminder preferenceTo make the App workOn your device only

Payments are processed by Apple; Rythmo never has access to your card details.

2. Ads and tracking

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Personalized ads are used only with your permission: through App Tracking Transparency on iOS and through Google's consent form in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland. If you decline, ads are not personalized. Rythmo Pro members see no ads at all, and the ad library does not load ads for them.

3. Notifications

Daily reminders are created on your device; no data is sent to a server for them. You can turn reminders off in the App under Profile > Settings or in your device settings.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

Your data is never sold and is not shared beyond the purposes above.

5. Retention and deletion

Your leaderboard entry is kept until you delete it. In the App, Profile > "Delete my progress and leaderboard entry" removes your leaderboard entry from the server and your progress from your device. Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on the device.

6. Children

The App is designed for healthcare students and professionals. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, correction and deletion under the GDPR and similar laws through the in-app delete option or the contact link below.

8. Medical disclaimer

Rythmo is for education only. In real patients, decisions and treatment follow current ACLS and national guidelines and the responsible physician.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: contact us on GitHub.
